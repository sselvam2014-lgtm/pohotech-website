Request Account Deletion

Instructions for requesting deletion of a KADAI MITRA account and associated information.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Users of KADAI MITRA can request deletion of their account and associated personal information by contacting POHO TECH using the support contact published on this page.

Before publishing: Add a monitored support email address below and implement an in-app account deletion path (for example, Settings → Account → Delete Account) before Google Play release if users can create accounts.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to SUPPORT_EMAIL_REPLACE_BEFORE_PUBLISH from an address where we can reply, or include your registered mobile number with country code.
  2. Use the subject line KADAI MITRA – Account Deletion Request.
  3. We may ask for reasonable verification to confirm that you own the account. Never send your OTP, UPI PIN or password.
  4. After verification, we will process the deletion request and tell you if any information must be retained by law.

What may be deleted

Depending on the App's implemented data model, deletion may cover account identifiers, profile details and business records associated with the account. The final scope must be aligned with the production database and any backups or legal retention duties.

Processing time

We will acknowledge the request and process it within the period required by applicable law. If additional verification or legally required retention applies, we will explain this to you.
