This page explains how users of the KADAI MITRA application can request deletion of their account.

1. How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your account, send an email to our support team using your registered mobile number and account details.

Support Email: pohotech.support@gmail.com

Send Deletion Request

2. Information That May Be Deleted

  • Account profile information.
  • Mobile number associated with the account.
  • Shop and business information.
  • Products and inventory records.
  • Sales and expense records.
  • Customer and order records associated with the account.

3. Data Retention

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable laws or for legitimate legal purposes. Such information will be retained only as necessary.

4. Processing Time

Our support team will review your request, verify account ownership and communicate the next steps. Deletion will be processed after verification, subject to applicable legal requirements.
